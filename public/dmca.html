Effective Date: July 23, 2026

1. Safe Harbor Statement

Hydro Sweep Services LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of our Services to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 ("DMCA"), 17 U.S.C. § 512, we will respond expeditiously to valid claims of copyright infringement, and we have adopted the policy set forth below with respect to any content that may be posted or transmitted through our Services by users. Nothing in this policy shall be construed as an admission of liability, and Hydro Sweep Services LLC reserves all defenses available under the DMCA's safe harbor provisions.

2. Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe that content accessible through our Services infringes your copyright, you (or your authorized agent) may submit a written notice ("Takedown Notice") to our Designated Agent that includes substantially the following:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf;
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list if multiple works are covered by a single notification;
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, including sufficient information to permit us to locate it (e.g., a URL);
  • Your contact information, including your name, address, telephone number, and email address;
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe that material you posted was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification to our Designated Agent containing:

  • Your physical or electronic signature;
  • Identification of the material that was removed and its location before removal;
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;
  • Your name, address, telephone number, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal district court in your district (or, if outside the U.S., any judicial district in which Hydro Sweep Services LLC may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original Takedown Notice.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, we may, in our discretion, restore the removed material within 10–14 business days unless the original complainant informs us that they have filed a court action seeking a restraining order.

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable law, Hydro Sweep Services LLC has adopted a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat infringers of the intellectual property rights of others.
