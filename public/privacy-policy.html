Hydro Sweep Services LLC ("Hydro Sweep Services," "we," "us," or "our") is a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Washington. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our chatbot, create an account, or make a purchase through our online store (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: email address, hashed password, and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Order Information: shipping address, billing details, and items purchased. Full payment card details are never transmitted to or stored on our servers — see Section 3 below.
  • Communications: messages you send us through our contact form or through our AI chatbot.
  • Usage Data: pages visited, referral source, device/browser information, and approximate location inferred from IP address, collected automatically for analytics and security purposes.

2. AI Chatbot Disclosure

Our website uses an artificial intelligence–powered chatbot to assist visitors with general inquiries and appointment booking requests. Conversations with the chatbot are logged and stored so that we can review service quality, respond to follow-up inquiries, and improve the chatbot's responses over time. Do not share sensitive personal information (such as financial account numbers, government identification numbers, or health information) in your chatbot conversation. The chatbot is an automated tool and does not constitute a binding confirmation of any appointment, service, or transaction until confirmed by a member of our team or through our official checkout process.

3. Payment Processing via Stripe

All payment transactions on our Services are processed by Stripe, Inc. ("Stripe"), a PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processor. When you make a purchase, your payment card information is transmitted directly to Stripe and is not stored on our servers. We receive limited transaction information from Stripe (such as the last four digits of your card, transaction amount, and billing name/email) necessary to fulfill your order, provide customer support, and maintain financial records. Stripe's own privacy practices are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account;
  • To process and fulfill orders, including assigning any purchased digital products or codes to your account;
  • To communicate with you about your orders, account, or inquiries;
  • To send transactional emails, such as order confirmations;
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you request deletion of your account (see Section 7), we will permanently delete your account and associated profile data from our active database, except where retention is required by law (for example, certain financial transaction records).

6. Sharing of Information

We share personal information only with service providers who perform services on our behalf (such as Stripe for payment processing and our email delivery provider for transactional emails), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Hydro Sweep Services LLC, our users, or the public.

7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may access and update your account information at any time by logging into your account. You may permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time from your Account Settings page. Once confirmed, this action is irreversible: your account record, profile information, and related personal data will be permanently purged from our active systems.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without verified parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your login session, remember your cart contents, and gather aggregate analytics about how our Services are used. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date." Your continued use of the Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any privacy rights described above, please contact us at info@hydrosweepservices.com.
