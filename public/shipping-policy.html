Shipping Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

This Shipping Policy applies to all orders placed through the Hydro Sweep Services LLC online store.

1. Order Processing Time

Orders are typically processed and prepared for shipment within 1–3 business days of payment confirmation. Processing times may be extended during high-volume periods or promotional sales, which will be noted on our site if applicable.

2. Shipping Methods & Costs

We currently offer free standard shipping on all orders. Estimated delivery time after processing is typically 3–7 business days within the United States, and 7–21 business days for select international destinations, depending on carrier and customs processing.

3. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email. You can also review your order and digital product assignment status anytime by logging into your account and visiting the Account page.

4. International Orders

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or fees levied by their destination country. These charges are determined by local customs authorities and are not included in the item price or shipping cost.

5. Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Packages

If your package appears lost, significantly delayed, or arrives damaged, please contact us at info@hydrosweepservices.com with your order number so we can investigate with the carrier and make it right.

6. Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for packages delayed or lost due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer. If you notice an error shortly after placing your order, contact us immediately and we will do our best to correct it before the order ships.

7. Contact

Questions about shipping? Reach us at info@hydrosweepservices.com or via our Contact page.
